# ahead® — Product Catalogue

*Source: www.ahead-nutrition.com, scraped April 2026*

---

## Overview

ahead® offers 170+ products across four main categories, all united by a single promise: **no added sugar, maximum taste**. Products are sold direct-to-consumer via their own Shopify store and through German retail. Price positioning is mid-market premium (€3.99–€29.99 for individual items, up to €32.99 for bundles).

Free shipping from €49. Free nut cream added at €79. Free choco chunks at €99.

## Bundling & Trial Packs

ahead has a strong "Probierpaket" (trial pack) strategy — almost every category has a curated taster set, typically 5–12 units priced at a slight discount vs. buying individual items. This lowers the barrier for new customers and drives volume.

**Bundle-Builder:** Customers can also create custom bundles via the Bundle-Builder feature (up to 20% saving).
